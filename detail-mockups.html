Aircraft detail pages

Drawn with the device's own font and the same coordinates as the firmware, pixel for pixel, on a 480 x 480 round screen. The photo is a drawn stand-in; real ones now arrive at 420 x 280 and are reduced to fit, so they are sharper than this.

Placeholder photo: ways to mark it

While the aircraft's own photo downloads, the page shows a stored photo of another aircraft of the same type (here with a red tail, to stand for a different airline). Each option below is a way of saying "this is not the real one yet". Options 2, 6 and 10 move.
