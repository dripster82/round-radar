Round Radar: mockups and UI flow

Design mockups for the 2.1" round screen (480×480), drawn at about life size on a desktop. The coastline, aircraft and QR code are made up for illustration. Nothing here is built yet.

UI flow

The device opens on whichever screen was last picked and stays there. It never switches by itself.

Gestures

GestureOn the scopeOn the clockOn aircraft detailOn settings
TapOpens the nearest aircraft (within about 3 mm)NothingCloses itPresses a button
Swipe sidewaysSteps the range: left for wider, right for closer (10, 25, 50, 100 km)NothingNothingNothing
Swipe up or downGoes to the clockGoes to the scopeNothingNothing
Long press (1 second)Opens settingsOpens settingsNothingNothing

The screen picked, the range and every setting are remembered through a restart.

Settings on the device

SettingChoicesDefault
Brightness10% to 100% in steps of 1070%
Clock12 hour or 24 hour24 hour
Night dimOff or On, with a From hour and an Until hour on its own page. When on, the screen drops to 10% between themOn, 22:00 to 07:00
PingOff or On. A short buzzer note when an aircraft comes within 5 kmOff
Wi-Fi & placeRestarts the phone setup. Saved details stay until new ones connect

The 5 km ping distance is fixed in this version. Settings and its night page always show at the chosen brightness, so they can be read at night.
